Titanic

View Description & Reviews

Titanic is a romantic disaster film about Jack and Rose who fall in love aboard the doomed RMS Titanic.

  • A timeless love story with powerful emotions.
  • Beautiful cinematography and music.
  • The ending still hits hard even after many years.

Interstellar

Interstellar follows astronauts travelling through a wormhole to find a new home for humanity.

  • One of the best space movies ever made.
  • The science concepts are fascinating.
  • The soundtrack and visuals are incredible.

Inception tells the story of thieves who enter people's dreams to steal secrets from their subconscious.

  • The spinning top ending is iconic.
